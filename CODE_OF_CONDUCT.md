# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in the **Hydrogen Team** community a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or experience level.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
- Demonstrating empathy, patience, and kindness toward other contributors
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive technical feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the open-source community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- Harassment, insults, derogatory comments, or personal attacks
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional technical community

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove or reject comments, commits, issues, or pull requests that violate this Code of Conduct.
